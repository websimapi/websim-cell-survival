# Cell Survival: Evolve or Die

A real-time multiplayer cell survival game where players control cells in a petri dish, consuming food and smaller cells to grow while avoiding larger predators.

## Controls

- **Mouse/Touch**: Move your cell in the direction of your cursor/finger
- **Space**: Split your cell (requires minimum size)
- **W key**: Eject mass (requires minimum size)
- **Double tap** (mobile): Split your cell

## Gameplay

- Consume smaller food particles (colored dots) to grow slightly
- Eat cells smaller than yours (you need to be at least 30% larger)
- Avoid cells larger than yours or you'll be consumed
- Green spiky viruses will split your cell if you're large enough
- When split, your cells will merge back together after a delay
- The larger you get, the slower you move

## Strategy Tips

- Split to catch escaping prey or to escape from larger predators
- Use viruses to hide when small, or to split large threats
- Ejecting mass can be used to feed allies or bait enemies
- Watch the leaderboard to know the biggest threats
- Try to trap smaller cells against the boundaries

Enjoy the game!
